# Hyperframes Composition Brief: DocExtract (narrated demo, v2)

## Objective
A complete narrated demo: the problem (manual extraction and review, nothing searchable, data lost in
thousands of documents), the solution (DocExtract, end to end), and a mission statement close.

## Output
- Composition: `composition/` (index.html is generated by `work/build.py` from `work/index.template.html`)
- Render: `brag.mp4`, 1920x1080, 30fps, ~102s (length set by narration at the user's request; no 25s cap)

## Source material
- Project: DocumentExtraction (README.md, frontend/src/theme.ts, pages and components for schema, upload, extraction review, query)
- Copy used verbatim: "Pick a schema, then add the documents to extract.", "Drag files here or click to browse",
  file-type hint, "Unreviewed", "Extracted", "Reviewed", "Schema saved.", "Ask about your documents…",
  empty-state suggestions, "Enter to send · Shift+Enter for a new line".
- Claims (all from README): local-only stack (Ollama, Qdrant, SQLite), OCR for scans, source quote per value,
  review before indexing, citations checked server-side.

## Creative direction
- Tone: polished; the problem act is quiet and dark (no music), the solution brings in the music.
- Problem visuals: animated illustrations built in HTML/canvas (document wall, retyping into a spreadsheet,
  page-flipping verification with a clock, failed search, the needed document sinking into the wall).
- Transition: the document wall collapses into the DocExtract mark.
- Demo: v1 screens (rebuilt from theme tokens), time-warped so each step holds for its narration line.
- Close: Capture → Extract → Search → Share lights up word by word with the voice, then the brand lockup.
  "Share" is framed as mission, not as a shipped feature.

## Visual identity
Primary #2563EB, accent on dark #56AAFF, stage #0A0B0C, paper #FFFFFF, canvas #F8FAFC, text #0F172A/#475569,
success #15803D, amber highlight #FBBF24. Inter + JetBrains Mono, served locally from `assets/fonts`.

## Audio
- Narration: Kokoro af_heart via `hyperframes tts`, one clip per line (`assets/vo/*.wav`), track 3, volume 1.
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (CC BY 4.0, Sascha Ende / ende.app). Enters at the reveal,
  beat-locked so its first beat (0.56s) lands on the brand; 0.14 under narration, 0.34 on the final lockup, 2.5s fade-out.
- SFX (Kenney CC0 + keyboard pack CC0): card sounds for paper, keypresses for typing (one per 2–3 chars),
  error buzz on "No results", clicks on cursor clicks, drops on items landing, bong on status changes,
  soft impact on the reveal and "Reviewed", heavy bell on the lockup.
- Audio-reactive visuals: skipped (the media-use extraction helper is not installed).

## Implementation notes
- One paused GSAP timeline drives a method-property tween that calls `render(t)` on every seek, so every frame is a pure function of time.
- `hyperframes check` passes (0 errors). Remaining warnings: small UI text below the phone-size type floor
  (it's a desktop app demo), and one contrast warning on a card mid-fade.
